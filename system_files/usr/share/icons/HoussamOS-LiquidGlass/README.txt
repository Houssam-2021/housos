HoussamOS Liquid Glass
Optical icon theme for the HoussamOS desktop.

Shells are clear glass: a cool rim on one edge, a warm rim on the other,
a soft blurred caustic through the body, and color pooled inside like light
in a lens. Marks are etched, not printed. Unknown applications get a
monogram rather than a copied trademark.

Install
  ./install.sh
or copy this folder to ~/.local/share/icons/HoussamOS-LiquidGlass
Then choose “HoussamOS Liquid Glass” in your desktop’s icon settings.
Anything not drawn here falls back to Breeze, then Adwaita.
